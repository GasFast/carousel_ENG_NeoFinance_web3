01 / The real problem
US tariffs.
Now what?
A European electric motorcycle company wants to sell in the US but tariffs block the path. The solution: the CKD model (Complete Knocked Down — bikes shipped disassembled and built at destination, earning a "Made in Mexico" stamp).
Europe produces batteries and components
Shipped disassembled to Mexico, assembled locally → "Made in Mexico"
Sold in the US tariff-free under USMCA

04 / The solution
On-demand tokenized
working capital
A €5M private credit pool, structured on blockchain as an infrastructure layer — not a speculative product.
1
Automatic drawdown triggered by verified production, Bill of Lading, or export invoice
2
Programmatic repayment upon confirmed US distributor payment
3
Private investors participate as lenders with transferable on-chain positions

06 / Risk & return
One cycle.
One repayment.
Each financing unit follows a single container of CKD bikes — ~€150k of goods — through its full commercial cycle.
1
Capital deployed when the container ships from Europe to Mexico
2
Bikes assembled in Mexico → sold & collected in the US (~3 months)
3
Full repayment of principal + yield only once distributor payments are received
Not token speculation. Short-duration, asset-backed private credit tied to real motorcycle sales and cash flows.
